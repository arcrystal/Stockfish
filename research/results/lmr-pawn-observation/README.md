# Pawn-history residual check, 20 September 2026

This extends the behavior-preserving LMR observer with the pawn-history entry read **before** making the move, plus in-check/excluded flags. All 64 searches again matched every baseline iteration score, PV, node count and final best move. The source patch is `../lmr-pawn-instrumentation.patch`; runner is `research/lmr_pawn_observe.py`. The unchanged first diagnostic binary is retained locally as `.research-tools/lmr-observation-v1`.

Among quiet, non-PV, non-check, non-excluded events with a genuinely reduced scout and an actual deeper verifier, a positive pawn-history entry had a small descriptive survival advantage after coarse stratification: +2.45 percentage points in roots 0–31 and +1.83 in roots 32–63. Strata were parent depth//2 capped at 8, reduction capped at 4, moveCount//4 capped at 4, statScore//4096 and cut-node flag. Only cells with at least ten events per pawn-sign group were included; weights were the smaller cell count. There were 56/57 matched cells with weight totals 1635/1553. Raw grouped counts are in summary.json.

The partition and bins are **exploratory**, not a preregistered held-out prediction experiment. These roots had already been examined for other features. Events share roots and subtrees; no independence or significance claim is made. The positive-pawn/nonpositive-statScore raw subgroup was worse in the first half and better in the second half. Pawn history already affects ordering and pruning, which select the observations. Most importantly, the diagnostic has no deeper labels for reduced fail-lows, where under-search could miss strong moves.

**Decision: no production LMR term from this result.** A small conditional association is not evidence that another pawn-history coefficient improves decisions. A stronger next study would sample reduced fail-lows in advance and replay the identical deterministic root prefix, changing only the selected scout depth, to audit actual missed thresholds. Replaying from a fresh FEN alone would not preserve TT, stack and learned-history state.
